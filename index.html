I study the sustainability of urban and infrastructure growth at the global scale, and develop geospatial methods to measure and forecast that change. My work projects where cities and road networks will expand and quantifies the cost in habitat, hazard exposure, and access to services.

How I work

Three habits that survive every project.

01

Take spatial structure seriously

Nearby places are not independent observations, and where an effect concentrates is often the finding itself.

02

Report the uncertainty honestly

Effect sizes, confidence, and sensitivity to assumptions travel with every headline number.

03

Validate against something I did not build

Results are checked against independently produced datasets, and disagreements are reported.
