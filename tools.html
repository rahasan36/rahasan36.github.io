Index/Tools

Tools I have built.

Geoprocessing tools designed and shipped through the Spatial Analyst extension of ArcGIS Pro, plus open-source utilities on GitHub.

Esri / ArcGIS Pro

Spatial Analyst geoprocessing tools.

T-01

Generate Weighted Voronoi

Weighted Voronoi tessellations sensitive to capacity or attractiveness. Validated on Cook County hospital service areas.
T-02

Network KDE

Network-constrained kernel density estimation with a companion Generate Lixels utility for line-event analyses on transportation networks.
T-03

Probabilistic Surface Parameters

Uncertainty propagation for terrain and density surfaces. Part of a three-tool sensitivity-analysis suite for raster workflows.
T-04

Probabilistic Kernel Density

Monte Carlo uncertainty quantification on point density estimates. Returns mean and confidence-interval rasters.
T-05

Raster Error Propagation

Tracks input uncertainty through map algebra expressions. Companion to the other two probabilistic tools.
T-06

Hansen Accessibility Index

Continuous accessibility surfaces from origins, destinations, and a chosen impedance function.
T-07

2SFCA Surface

Two-step floating catchment area surfaces for service-availability analysis. Common in health-access and food-access research.
T-08

Huff Model Probability Surface

Spatial interaction probabilities under the classic Huff formulation. Useful for retail and service-area modeling.
T-09

Volume Percentile Contours

Density-based percentile contours for 3D point clouds and time-aware kernel surfaces. Featured in the 2024 Dev Summit plenary.
